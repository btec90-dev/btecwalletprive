3. CURRENCY, TWT & DATA PERSISTENCE RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Currencies (data/model/Currency.kt):
- Supported: USD ($) and CFA ("#,##0.00 CFA" — never "FCFA"; legacy FCFA/XOF automatically normalized to CFA)
- TWT Dynamic Rules (TwtAccountHelper in data/model/Account.kt):
  * Any account containing "TWT" dynamically normalizes to "TWT <CurrentYear>" (e.g., TWT 2026)
  * Type: SAVINGS | Avatar: 🪙 | Currency strictly locked to USD ($)
  * Selecting TWT auto-defaults category to "Bonus"

Local Database (Room - WalletDatabase.kt):
- Tables: accounts, transactions, recurring_transactions, contacts
- Custom categories persisted in SharedPreferences & synced to Firestore

Cloud Sync & Firestore Isolation (firestore.rules & CloudSyncManager.kt):
- Database ID: ai-studio-android-balatecs-4baf2e8b-4fe1-4d7a-a144-fd45aa8e45ca
- Shared Data (both 410bala@gmail.com & traore200@gmail.com):
  * shared_wallets/balatec_shared/{accounts, transactions, recurring, categories}
  * Strictly forbids TWT accounts/transactions (rejected by rules & client filters)
- Private TWT Data (410bala@gmail.com Primary Admin ONLY):
  * private_twt/admin_only/{accounts, transactions, recurring}
  * Completely hidden from traore200@gmail.com across sync, UI, totals, Quick-Add, and PDF/JSON/CSV exports
